HOW IT WORKS

Simple by design.
Useful in real life.

No complicated marketplace. Just the right food, in the right hands, at the right time.

01

Businesses list

Restaurants publish safe surplus — food name, quantity, pickup time, address, and pricing: free or low-cost total.

02

NGOs discover

Browse live surplus. See quantity, exact address, pickup deadline and total price — before reserving anything.

03

Reserve quantity

Choose exactly how many plates your organisation needs. A unique pickup code is generated instantly.

04

Pickup assigned

The restaurant assigns a pickup person. Status updates track the journey from assigned → on the way → arrived.

05

OTP → rescued

The NGO shows their pickup code. Restaurant verifies it. Food rescued — impact recorded immediately.
